# Terms of use

Short version: Hark is free, open source and runs only on your Mac. Use it however you like, at your own risk.

## The deal

- Hark is free software under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use, copy, change and share it, including commercially.
- It comes **as is**. No warranty, no guarantee it works for your use case, and nobody is liable if something goes wrong, like a wrong transcription, a lost clipboard item or text pasted in the wrong place.
- Don't rely on it for anything critical (medical, legal, safety) without checking what it typed.

## Your data

- Hark doesn't collect anything. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no network calls.
- Your voice is processed on your Mac and thrown away right after. Nothing is saved to disk.
- The text ends up wherever your cursor was, and that app's own privacy policy applies from there.

## Permissions

Hark asks for Microphone and Accessibility. It only uses them to hear you while you hold the key, notice the key and paste the text. You can turn any of them off anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

## Recording others

If you dictate in a room with other people, you're responsible for following local rules about recording people. Hark only listens while you hold the key and keeps nothing, but it's still your mic.
